# YC Pitch Deck Content Framework

The 12 standard YC pitch deck slides. Based on Kevin Hale and Geoff Ralston partner frameworks.

## The Golden Rule
Every slide should make the investor think: "I need to meet this team immediately."

## Slide-by-Slide Guide

### 1. Title
- Company name in large bold text
- One-line: what you do (plain language, no jargon)
- Optional: logo top-right

### 2. Problem
- The pain: who has it, how bad is it, what do they do today?
- Quantify: $ lost / hours wasted / % of market affected
- End with: "There must be a better way."

### 3. Solution
- What you built
- How it solves the problem
- Key metric: X% improvement, Y hours saved, Z $ saved
- Screenshot or demo if visual

### 4. Why Now
- Why couldn't this have been built 5 years ago?
- Technology shift? Regulation change? Market timing?
- Show you understand the tailwind

### 5. Market Size
- BOTTOMS-UP TAM (not top-down fantasy)
- TAM → SAM → SOM with specific numbers
- Example: "$2.3B TAM (500K businesses × $4,600/yr)"

### 6. Product
- How it works (3-step flow)
- Architecture or screenshot
- Key features that matter, not every feature

### 7. Traction
- Revenue or user growth curve (hockey stick)
- Absolute numbers AND growth rate
- Notable logos or customers
- "We're growing faster than [benchmark] at the same stage"

### 8. Business Model
- How you make money: SaaS, marketplace, transaction, etc.
- Pricing: $ per month/user/deal
- Unit economics: CAC, LTV, gross margin
- Path to $1M ARR, $10M ARR

### 9. Competition
- 2x2 matrix (incumbents vs you on key dimensions)
- Table comparison
- Your wedge: what only you can do
- "Our competitors are [A] and [B]. We're different because..."

### 10. Team
- Founders + key hires: name, title, relevant past
- Why THIS team will win
- Past exits, domain expertise, unique credentials
- "We've been working on this problem for X years"

### 11. Financials
- Revenue projections (conservative, base, aggressive)
- Burn rate and runway
- Capital efficiency: how far does $1 go?
- 3-year summary table

### 12. Ask
- How much: $X
- What for: X% engineering, Y% sales, Z% marketing
- Milestones: what will the company achieve in 18 months?
- "With $X we will reach Y metric and be ready for Series A"
